DEPARTMENT OF HIGHER EDUCATION & STUDENT EMPOWERMENT
NATIONAL MERIT SCHOLARSHIP SCHEME (ONGOING DISBURSEMENT GUIDELINES)

POLICY MEMORANDUM: GENERAL SCHOLARSHIP ELIGIBILITY & AADHAAR SEEDING NORMS

This notice is issued for the information of all registered college students who have been provisionally sanctioned scholarship benefits under the State Merit Assistance Scheme.

ELIGIBILITY AND MAINTENANCE CRITERIA:
1. Beneficiaries must maintain a minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 6.5 or 60% aggregate marks across consecutive academic terms.
2. The scholarship will be directly credited into the student's active bank account via the Public Financial Management System (PFMS) through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
3. The bank account must be an individual savings account (joint accounts with parents/guardians are invalid) and must be seeded with Aadhaar with NPCI mapping enabled.

IMPORTANT NOTES:
- This is an ongoing standing policy guideline.
- Applications and maintenance updates are processed on a rolling basis throughout the financial year as funds are sanctioned by the state treasury. No fixed application deadline or cutoff date applies to existing beneficiaries under this policy memorandum.
- Students must notify their institution immediately if they transfer to another institution or discontinue their studies.

CAUTIONARY ADVICE:
- Submitting an account without NPCI active status will lead to automatic rejection of the remittance transaction without prior alert.
- Do not share your portal login credentials or OTP with anyone offering to 'fast-track' your disbursement.

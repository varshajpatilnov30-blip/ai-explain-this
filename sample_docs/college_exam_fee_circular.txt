CIRCULAR: SUBMISSION OF EXAMINATION APPLICATION FORM FOR NOVEMBER-DECEMBER 2026 END-SEMESTER EXAMINATIONS (REGULAR & ARREARS)

It is hereby notified for information of all undergraduate and postgraduate students (B.Tech / B.E. / M.Tech / MBA / MCA) appearing for the upcoming End-Semester Examinations scheduled in November-December 2026 that the examination fee portal will be activated on the University ERP portal (erp.apexuniv.ac.in).

All concerned candidates are directed to adhere to the following schedule strictly:
1. Online submission of exam form and fee payment without fine: Up to October 24, 2026 (11:59 PM).
2. Online submission of exam form with late fine of Rs. 500/-: From October 25, 2026 to October 28, 2026 (5:00 PM).
3. No examination forms shall be entertained under any circumstances after October 28, 2026 (5:00 PM).

FEES STRUCTURE:
- Theory papers: Rs. 250/- per paper
- Practical / Laboratory / Viva-Voce: Rs. 350/- per paper
- Marksheet fee: Rs. 150/- (mandatory for all regular candidates)

MANDATORY INSTRUCTIONS & PRECAUTIONS:
a) Minimum attendance criterion of 75% aggregate is mandatory to generate the admit card / hall ticket. Students below 75% attendance will be detained automatically by the system.
b) Ensure transaction receipt is downloaded and retained. Payment deducted from bank account but not reflected on ERP portal must be reported to the Accounts Section within 24 hours with UTR number.
c) Students with pending library dues or hostel clearance will not be permitted to complete the exam registration until clearance certificate is uploaded.
d) Hard copy submission is NOT required at the university office; however, one copy must be submitted to the respective Department HoD office for internal record keeping before November 02, 2026.
